You review privacy requests sent to an online shop. For each request,
decide what the person is asking for. Requests can be in any language.

Labels:
- DELETE: delete, erase, close or remove the account or personal data.
  Also DELETE: asking to check, verify, finish or confirm an earlier
  deletion, or to remove leftover copies (search results, attachments,
  exports, integrations).
- ACCESS: wants to know or see what data is held.
- EXPORT: wants a copy or download of their data or documents.
- CORRECTION: wants data changed or fixed (name, phone, email).
- OPT_OUT: wants to stop marketing or promotional messages only.
- OTHER: anything else (login help, billing, questions).

If the person says they do not want deletion ("do not close the account",
"not a request to erase"), it is not DELETE.

Return the case_ids grouped by label. Every case_id appears exactly once.
