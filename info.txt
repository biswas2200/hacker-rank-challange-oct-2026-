The three step phases mean:

- FINDING is "here is the problem."
- REMEDIATION is "here is the fix."
- VERIFICATION is "check that this is true afterwards."

VERIFY_RECORD_ABSENT (your case_06 question) means "go look and confirm this record no longer exists." It is a check, not something that already happened. In the INCOMPLETE case above, evidence_id is empty on the fix and the check because they haven't happened yet. Only findings point at real evidence.
